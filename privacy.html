MathSnap SAT ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Photos and Camera: When you use the Snap & Solve feature, images are captured and sent to our AI service to generate a solution. Images are processed in real time and are not stored on our servers.
  • Learning Progress: Your XP, completed lessons, and quiz results are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data does not leave your device unless you are signed in to a cloud account.
  • Account Information: If you create an account, we store your email address and progress data securely in our database (Supabase).
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage data (e.g., which lessons are most used) to improve the app. This data cannot be used to identify you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered math solving and quiz generation
  • To track your learning progress and award XP
  • To send optional daily practice reminders (only if you enable notifications)
  • To improve app features based on anonymous usage patterns

Camera and Photo Library

Camera and photo library access is used solely to photograph math problems for AI solving. Images are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for processing and are not stored, sold, or shared with any other third party.

Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic: Powers AI math solving and quiz generation. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Stores account and progress data securely. Subject to Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages in-app purchases and subscriptions. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

MathSnap SAT is designed for high school students preparing for the SAT. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Retention

Local data (progress, XP) is stored on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app. Account data is retained until you request deletion.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
